# NotiFlow – FAQ

Welcome to the NotiFlow Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ).  
This guide covers compatibility, security, and pricing with helpful visuals.  

---

## 📦 Platforms & Compatibility  

**Q: What operating systems does NotiFlow support?**  
A: NotiFlow works on **Windows 10+, macOS 11+**, and modern browsers like Chrome, Firefox, and Edge.  

![Platforms](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/SabehMughal/SabehMughal/main/Images/NotiFlow_FAQ_Platforms.png.png)



**Q: Can I use NotiFlow on mobile?**  
A: Yes, mobile apps are available on the **App Store (iOS)** and **Google Play (Android)**.  

**Q: Does NotiFlow sync across devices?**  
A: Absolutely. Any action you take on one device is instantly reflected across all others.  

---

### 🔒 Security & Privacy  

**Q: Is my data secure in NotiFlow?**  
A: Yes. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. NotiFlow complies with GDPR and HIPAA standards.  

[![Security & Privacy](https://github.com/SabehMughal/SabehMughal/raw/662994a613205816392581fa9cfd9b1f7734efe6/NotiFlow_FAQ_Security_and_Privacy.png)](https://github.com/SabehMughal/SabehMughal/raw/662994a613205816392581fa9cfd9b1f7734efe6/NotiFlow_FAQ_Security_and_Privacy.png)


**Q: Does NotiFlow support SSO (Single Sign-On)?**  
A: Yes. NotiFlow supports SSO integrations with Google Workspace and Microsoft Azure.
